# Reckon Aura Theme: a Desk theme for ERPNext 16

Reckon Aura Theme is the Desk theme for ERPNext 16 used to give Reckon products a consistent look and feel. A good ERP should be pleasant to use every day, and the theme is part of how [ReckonERP](https://app.reckon.tech) presents its workspaces.

## Detailed features

### Theme for the ERPNext Desk
- A Frappe Desk theme built for ERPNext 16
- Applies one consistent look across the Desk, so Reckon modules feel like one product
- Works as a separate app, so it can be updated without touching ERP data

### Part of the Reckon experience
- Pairs with Reckon modules such as ReckonBooks, distribution, HRMS and CRM
- Supports the SaaS signup and public pages that sit around the ERP

## Built on Frappe and ERPNext

The theme is a native Frappe app for ERPNext 16, so it follows the framework's Desk structure rather than replacing it.

## Who it is for

Businesses subscribing to ReckonERP, and teams who want their ERPNext Desk to look consistent and clean.
